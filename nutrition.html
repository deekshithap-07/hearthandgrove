Cashews & almonds benefits

Nuts bring healthy fats, plant protein, fibre, and key minerals in one simple portion. Cashews offer a creamy, mineral-rich bite; almonds deliver vitamin E and a satisfying crunch — both support steady energy without fuss.

  • Fit easily into snacks, salads, and cooking
