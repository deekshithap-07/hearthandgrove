Our story

It started with one honest handful.

We wanted nuts that tasted real — clean, full of flavour, and easy to trust. So we built Hearth & Grove around that idea: find the best cashews and almonds, pack them with care, and bring them to the tables we share.

From grove to hearth, nothing fancy gets in the way. Quality over noise. Connection over complication. Every pack is meant to feel grounding — something you reach for without thinking twice.

About us

What we stand for

We exist to make premium nuts feel approachable — rooted in care, clear in quality, and always ready for everyday life.

Our mission

Bring carefully sourced cashews and almonds into everyday kitchens — nourishing, honest, and free of fuss.

How we choose

We look for freshness, flavour, and clarity first. If it doesn’t taste right or feel right, it doesn’t make the pack.

Who it’s for

Anyone who wants real food on the table — for quiet snacks, shared meals, or the small rituals that keep you going.
