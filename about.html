About Us

We believe in the quiet power of real food — nourishing tables, one handful at a time.

Established with care.

Hearth & Grove began with a love for honest food: nuts that taste like they came from somewhere real. We source cashews and almonds with intention, pack them with care, and share them with the people we cook and gather with.

From grove to hearth, we keep value close to the source — choosing quality over noise, and connection over complication. Every pack is meant to feel grounding, clean, and ready for everyday tables.

We believe nourishment should feel simple: premium nuts, thoughtfully presented, and rooted in the places and people behind them.
